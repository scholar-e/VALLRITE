Normalized mouth landmarks

Measurements overlay the source video and update with playback. Rounding and closure are geometry proxies, not probabilities. Additional measurements describe projected opening area, side gaps, asymmetry, and lip thickness; these are exploratory, not validated phoneme discriminators. The expandable landmark view uses eye-distance units; it is not positioned on the face.

Video and frame controls share the clip’s timestamps. Outside the exported data range, the nearest recorded frame stays visible. The sample measurements cover the first two seconds and use a manual speaker region.
